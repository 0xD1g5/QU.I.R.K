The readiness score no longer rises when a scan observes more healthy endpoints. Each weakness now costs points according to how many endpoints or certificates carry it, saturating at 40, instead of according to its share of the scan, so a deeper or wider scan of the same estate cannot dilute a problem away. Scan errors are counted the same way. RSA, ECDSA and EdDSA certificate keys are all treated as quantum-vulnerable and cost the same agility penalty; the old credit for ECDSA is gone, and only a post-quantum key exchange or an ML-DSA, SLH-DSA or composite certificate key earns agility credit, once. The "Plan ECDSA adoption" roadmap item is now "Plan post-quantum (PQC/hybrid) certificate adoption". A Kerberos result that recorded no encryption types no longer counts the identity domain as assessed. Upgrade note: the scoring version is now 4.0, and 4.0 scores are not comparable with v3 scores. The reference calibration estates score the same as before, small estates score somewhat higher, and estates whose score was propped up by many healthy endpoints score lower; the dashboard rescores past scans with 4.0, while saved reports and intelligence files keep the version they were written with. QRAMM: the overall score now averages only the dimensions that were assessed, instead of counting an unassessed dimension as 0, and shows how many of the four were assessed ("N of 4 dimensions assessed"); a dimension answered after the score was saved shows "Stale — recalculate" instead of "Self-assessed" until Calculate Score is run again. A QRAMM score saved before the upgrade shows "Recalculate to apply the updated overall" instead of a maturity level and marks its answered dimensions "Stale — recalculate" until the session is scored again.
