The QRAMM assessment surfaces now tell the truth about what was assessed. The dashboard's Compliance Map tab refreshes its table after a successful Calculate Score instead of staying on the unscored view, and announces "Compliance map updated." to screen readers. Answered SGRM, DPE and ITR practice areas, which used to show no score at all, now show their scores with a "Self-assessed" marker on the dashboard and in the PDF, so a reader can tell them from scanner-measured CVI rows; the PDF's per-framework coverage tier is replaced by counts of Scanner-measured, Self-assessed and Not assessed practice areas. A dimension with no answered question, CVI included, now reads "Not assessed" on the Scorecard, the Compliance Map and the PDF instead of maturity level 1 or a score of 0.0, and is left out of the Maturity Distribution. The print/PDF QRAMM page loads again for a scored session: it used to request the score from a POST-only route, so the page never finished loading and the PDF export timed out. `quirk doctor`, `quirk qramm` and `quirk cve` now print their own help at every level without running anything, and reject an unknown action or extra argument with exit status 2; `quirk cve status --format=json` now emits JSON, and an invalid `--format` value is rejected. Scripts that passed extra arguments to `quirk doctor` must drop them. The QRAMM score itself is unchanged.
