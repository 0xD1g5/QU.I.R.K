`if _ceiling is not None and total_score > _ceiling`, so compression applied only ABOVE the
ceiling. A piecewise linear compression is not order-preserving. Measured with ceiling 84: a
computed 84 emitted 84, a computed 85 emitted 71, and nothing in the whole range [85, 100] ever
emitted more than 84 — **every well-run estate scored below a mediocre one.**
`test_identity_surface::test_weak_kerberos_lowers_score` caught it as a live P1 violation (adding
three weak-Kerberos findings moved an estate 77 -> 84). The handoff had listed that test among the
11 "pinned values D's curve moved". It was not a stale pin. It was right.

Fixed by compressing across the whole range. **The ladder did not move at all** — all five rungs
already computed above their own ceilings, which is exactly why a five-rung ladder could not see
this and a property test could. The blocking constraint's own mitigation was re-run: lift +1/+2
(non-zero, so not a clamp), profiles 16/18/19 (three distinct), controls reproduce 100 and 18.
