<objective>
Build the config and storage foundation for SNMPv3: a per-host `SnmpV3Credential` dataclass
(env-var-name secrets, D-01), a `ConnectorsCfg.snmp_v3_credentials` field with a host-keyed loader
that validates SHA/AES-only protocols at load time (D-02), and the three additive `HardwareDevice`
columns that persist the negotiated version/protocol metadata.

Purpose: Provides the typed config surface every downstream plan consumes and the ORM columns the
three projection sites will read. Storing env-var NAMES (not secrets) is the primary leakage control.
The SHA/AES protocol allowlist defined here is the exact key set 139-02's probe protocol-map keys off
of — it is a live contract, not dead validation.
Output: `SnmpV3Credential` importable from `quirk.config`; three migrated ORM columns.
</objective>
