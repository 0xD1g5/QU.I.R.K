<objective>
Build the title-translation layer D-06 puts in scope, and the gate that keeps it honest.

Purpose: the dashboard's finding titles and the CLI pipeline's finding titles are two
independently-maintained vocabularies for the same endpoint conditions, and
`RemediationItemFingerprint` rows are written only from the CLI's vocabulary
(`remediation_persist.py:254` hashing `evaluate_endpoints()` output). Computing a fingerprint from
the dashboard's own title silently fails to match for most classes, which renders as a FALSE "not
mapped to a theme" (A1). The opposite failure — translating to the wrong CLI title — renders a
confidently WRONG theme, which is worse than no theme. Everything downstream in this phase joins
through this module, so it ships first, alone, with its own gate.

Output: `quirk/dashboard/api/finding_title_bridge.py` (pure functions, no DB, no I/O) and
`tests/test_finding_title_bridge.py`.

Hard constraints:

  diverge, 3 match verbatim" and flags its own confidence as MEDIUM on this join. Re-verify the
  mapping **class by class**, reading both generators, and record what you actually find. A
  mismatch between your finding and the research count is a FINDING to record in the SUMMARY, not
