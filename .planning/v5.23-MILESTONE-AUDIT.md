## What this milestone shipped

Four phases that chain rather than stand alone: fractional scores made to survive the transports (199),
operator-controlled report branding and template overrides on a sandboxed Jinja2 env (200), a
remediation roadmap where every item carries a real second-scoring-pass score-lift and the aggregate is
one independent rescore deliberately *not* a sum (201), and a per-finding storyline drawer consuming
that lift at theme level (202).

Two locked decisions were falsified by research *before* planning, which is the milestone's best
process outcome: `FindingItem.id` turned out to be `CryptoEndpoint.id` (2–4 findings share one), so the
drawer's endpoint could not have identified a finding; and 41% of fingerprints proved multi-theme, so
"the owning theme" was never a data-model invariant.

   templates; that scoping is currently undocumented.
3. **INT-02 / truncation** — needs an explicit truncate vs round vs render-fractional decision.
