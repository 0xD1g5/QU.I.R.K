## The `phase.complete` integrity question

Phase 186.1 proved live that `phase.complete` marks a phase complete without checking its own
plans are done. The cross-phase question that raises: **could earlier v5.19 phases have been
closed by that same buggy verb, making their completion claims untrustworthy?**

**No evidence of that, checked three independent ways:**

1. **Disk evidence** — the bug's signature is a phase marked complete with SUMMARYs missing
   relative to PLANs. All 10 phases show plans == summaries exactly.
2. **Tracking prose** — no impossible aggregate (`142`-style) appears anywhere in ROADMAP.md or
   REQUIREMENTS.md; each phase's checkbox cites an internally-consistent plan count and date.
3. **186.1's own conduct** — it documented the bug against itself and deliberately hand-wrote its
   close instead of calling the verb.

**Honest limit:** this does not *prove* the verb was never invoked on an earlier phase — that
cannot be reconstructed retroactively without running the (forbidden) mutating verb. It proves the
corruption signature is absent everywhere checkable, which is the strongest available evidence.
