# 999.115 — severity caps the NUMBER, not only the band

**Status: DECIDED by the operator, 2026-09-14.** This decision **supersedes Phase 184.4 D-03**.

D-03 is not deleted and must not be. It was correct reasoning about a real problem, it shipped a
mechanism that is still in place, and the record of why it was chosen is what makes this reversal
legible. It is superseded, with its rationale preserved below.

---

## What D-03 said, and why it was reasonable

Phase 184.4 D-01/D-02/D-03 established that an open CRITICAL finding floors the emitted **band** at
FAIR, and that the **number never moves**. The separation was deliberate and had two good arguments
behind it:

1. **No double-counting.** `agility_high_impact_ratio` already moved the number for
   HIGH+CRITICAL findings. Capping the number as well would have counted the same evidence twice.
2. **Comparability.** A score that means the same thing across scans is easier to trend, and a
   band is the right place for a policy judgement that is not arithmetic.

`tests/test_scoring_orthogonal_contract.py::test_severity_floor_caps_band_not_score` was written as
the executable form of it, explicitly to "fail loudly if a future contributor … moves the cap onto
`total_score` instead of `rating`". **It did exactly that, on the first attempt, which is the
system working.**

## Why it is superseded

D-03's separation is what produced the product's central incoherence. Measured 2026-09-14 on the
31-host purpose-built vulnerable estate:

- the number said **87** — numerically EXCELLENT, since the threshold is 85
- the label said **FAIR**, because `cap_band_for_severity()` overrode it

Two statements about one estate, disagreeing, with the number being the half that survives into a
slide deck, a spreadsheet, or a client's memory. 999.115 records the observation that decides it:
*"most people will see 87 and say not bad."*

The compensation D-03 introduced is evidence for, not against, this change. A band cap exists
**because** the number was already known to be wrong; it treats the symptom at the presentation
layer while leaving the arithmetic saying something no consultant would say. C moves the correction
to where the defect is.

Three further findings, all measured rather than argued
(`999.115-scoring-model-candidate-measurements.md`):

- **Nothing else works.** Candidate A (larger weights) spreads the scale but saturates subscores at
  0/25; candidate D (non-linear curve) never fixes ladder monotonicity at any exponent and never
  moves R2. Only an absolute consequence term moves R3/R4/R5 into their operator-assigned bands.
- **D-03's own no-double-counting argument has since been undermined from the other side.**
  `agility_high_impact_ratio` — the "the ratio already moves the number" half of the argument — is
  itself being removed in this same change set, because as a *prevalence* measure it dilutes as its
  denominator grows and is the sole remaining cause of P1 (adding MEDIUM/LOW findings raising the
  score). Once it is gone there is no double count to avoid, because there is no other path by which
  consequence reaches the number at all.
- **Comparability is preserved deliberately, by a different mechanism.** `SCORING_VERSION` exists
  for exactly this, and is bumped as part of this change so every report surface discloses that
  pre- and post-change scores are not comparable. That is the honest form of D-03's second argument —
  disclose the discontinuity rather than avoid it forever.

## What replaces it

`_consequence_ceiling()` in `quirk/intelligence/scoring.py`: an absolute ceiling keyed to the COUNT
of open CRITICAL/HIGH findings, applied to `total_score` **before** the band is derived, so the
number and the label cannot disagree again.

Two properties of the implementation are load-bearing and were established by measurement:

- **Thresholds are derived, not chosen.** "Cap at POOR" *is* `FAIR - 1`; "cap at FAIR" *is*
  `MODERATE - 1`, read from `BAND_THRESHOLDS` via `_top_of_band()`. One free parameter remains,
  `DEEP_CRITICAL_CEILING = 25`, sourced from the operator's R5 rung.
- **The score is COMPRESSED into `[0, ceiling]`, never clamped to it.** A hard `min(score, ceiling)`
  was implemented first and measured: it made the score inert on capped estates — renewing every
  expired certificate moved it +0, and the three calibration profiles collapsed to one number. The
  remediation roadmap would have shown "+0 points" against every recommendation the product makes.
  Compression bounds the maximum identically while preserving ordering, lift and profile
  sensitivity. **This distinction is not a refinement; without it the change is a regression.**

`cap_band_for_severity()` **stays**. It is now usually a no-op — the number has already fallen into
the band it would have forced — but it remains correct as defence in depth, and removing it would
discard a second independent guarantee for no benefit.

## Consequences accepted

- Every score the product has ever emitted moves. `SCORING_VERSION` is bumped; golden fixtures are
  regenerated.
- **Lift granularity shrinks inside the capped region** — a 4-point raw gain reads as +1 once
  compressed. Directionally correct and non-zero, but coarser. Accepted.
- Four tests besides the contract test encoded D-03's invariant and are rewritten to assert the new
  one, each keeping the old expectation quoted in its docstring so the reversal stays visible at the
  site rather than only here.

## Standing caveat, unchanged

The calibration ladder has five rungs, four of them synthetic. The **shape** — that consequence must
be absolute rather than proportional — is well evidenced. The **constants** are a first fit. Add
real-scan rungs before treating the thresholds as settled.
