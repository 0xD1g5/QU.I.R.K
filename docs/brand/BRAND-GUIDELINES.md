# QU.I.R.K. Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · 2026-10-01**

These rules cover the QU.I.R.K. logo system, its colours and type, and how the brand sits next to client and consultancy branding. Every asset is generated by [`build_logo.py`](build_logo.py). The interactive companion page is [`brand-sheet.html`](brand-sheet.html).

---

## 1. The idea

QU.I.R.K. finds the cryptography running in an environment and judges whether it will survive a quantum adversary. The logo is a **scanner lens over a lattice**. The lens stands for the inventory work, and the lattice stands for the post-quantum cryptography it looks for.

The name is the thesis. Lattice cryptography (ML-KEM, ML-DSA) is secure because of a **small, deliberate error**. Take the error away and the scheme becomes linear algebra that anyone can solve. That error is the quirk. In the logo, it is the one point drawn in the accent colour, sitting just off its place in the grid.

Six post-quantum references are built into the geometry. They are listed in §4. They should reward a curious engineer without getting in a buyer's way.

## 2. Logo system

**Rule zero: whenever the name is spelled out, the lens is the Q.** Never put the mark next to a wordmark that has its own Q.

| Asset | Files | Use | Minimum size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary logo** | `quirk-logo.svg`, `-dark` | Default for the README, dashboard header, report footers and slides | 24 px tall |
| **Primary + descriptor** | `quirk-logo-descriptor.svg`, `-dark` | First use in a document, cover pages, proposals | 48 px tall |
| **Display logo** | `quirk-logo-display.svg`, `-dark` | Signage, title slides, swag. The lens is 1.55× the cap height | 80 px tall |
| **Hero** | `quirk-hero.svg`, `-dark` (1600×900) | Report covers, banners, GitHub/PyPI social preview, booth backdrop | 960 px wide |
| **Mark** | `quirk-mark.svg`, `-dark`, `-mono` | App icon, avatar, collapsed sidebar, anywhere the name already appears nearby | 48 px |
| **Favicon** | `quirk-favicon.svg`, `-dark` | Browser tabs and other sizes under 48 px | 16 px |

The logo is **responsive**: the larger it is shown, the more detail it carries.

```
16 px     favicon      ring + tail + error point
48 px     mark         + 3×3 lattice + ghost node
24 px+    logo         lens-as-Q + U.I.R.K.
80 px+    display      oversized lens
960 px+   hero         + 256-tick dial + lattice field across the canvas
```

### Choosing a version

- Is the name already visible nearby (a page title, a product name)? Use the **mark**.
- Is it the first time a reader meets the brand in this document? Use **primary + descriptor**.
- Is it a large surface seen from a distance? Use **display** or **hero**.
- Otherwise, use the **primary logo**.

## 3. Construction

All dimensions are on the master mark's 120-unit artboard. Do not redraw the logo by hand. Change `build_logo.py` and regenerate, because the easter eggs only hold if the geometry stays exact.

| Element | Specification |
|---|---|
| Ring | 256-gon, centreline radius 36, stroke 10. Vertices at angles (2k+1)·π/256 |
| Split | Two halves along the 45° diagonal. The top-left seam is open (3 vertices dropped each side). The bottom-right seam is closed by the tail |
| Tail | Radial at 45°, starting at the ring's inner edge, running 26 units past the centreline. Butt caps |
| Lattice | Basis b₁ = (0.31r, 0), b₂ = (0.36·0.31r, 0.31r). 3×3 nodes, with the centre node replaced |
| Error point | Offset e = (0.36s, −0.24s) from the centre node (s = 0.31r). Radius 1.3× a lattice dot. Signal colour |
| Ghost node | Outline at the true centre node, 50% opacity. Leave it out of the mono version |
| Wordmark | Monoline geometric caps, stroke 0.175 × cap height, clipped flat to the cap band. The periods are round lattice dots |
| Lens as Q | Outer diameter 1.04 × cap (round overshoot). The tail drops below the baseline as the Q's descender |

### Clear space

Keep at least **one ring-stroke width** clear on every side of the mark (10 units on the 120 grid). For the logo, use the **height of the U.I.R.K. letter stroke × 2**. Measure from the tail tip and the outer edge of the ring.

## 4. The easter eggs

Use this section to brief anyone who will present the brand. In a pitch, mention one egg (usually #4) and let the audience ask about the rest.

| # | What you see | What it encodes | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A ring that reads as a circle | A 256-gon at the roots of X²⁵⁶+1, the polynomial ring R_q = Z_q[X]/(X²⁵⁶+1) | FIPS 203, FIPS 204 |
| 2 | Two ring halves joined only by the tail | Hybrid key exchange: a classical and a post-quantum shared secret fed into one KDF | X25519MLKEM768 |
| 3 | 3×3 sheared dot grid | The k×k public matrix A of ML-KEM-768 (k = 3), drawn in a skewed "bad" basis | FIPS 203 |
| 4 | Centre point nudged off its node | Learning With Errors: b = As + e. The error is the quirk | MLWE |
| 5 | Ink `#0D0124` | 0x0D01 = 3329, the ML-KEM modulus q | FIPS 203 |
| 6 | Signal `#7FE001` | 0x7FE001 = 8,380,417 = 2²³ − 2¹³ + 1, the ML-DSA modulus q | FIPS 204 |
| + | Hero only: 256 ticks around the lens, every 32nd long | The same 256 roots, made explicit on the largest format | — |

**Not encoded:** SLH-DSA (FIPS 205), FN-DSA (draft FIPS 206), HQC. Don't claim them. A Merkle-fork treatment of the K is a candidate for a future version.

**Plain-text breadcrumb.** Every generated SVG carries the parameters in its `<desc>`, so anyone who opens the file finds the hint.

## 5. Colour

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#0D0124` | Logo on light grounds. Dark ground for the brand |
| **Paper** | `#F4F5F9` | Logo on dark grounds. Light ground for the brand |
| **Signal** | `#7FE001` | The error point. **Nothing else** |

### Contrast (WCAG 2.x)

| Pair | Ratio | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Ink on Paper | 18.4 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Ink on white | 20.1 : 1 | AAA, any size |
| Signal on Ink | 12.0 : 1 | AAA, usable for text on dark grounds |
| Signal on Paper | 1.53 : 1 | **Fail.** Fine as a graphic dot, never use for text |

### Rules

- **Signal appears once per logo, on the error point.** Don't recolour the ring, the lattice, the wordmark or UI chrome with it.
- **Signal is not a UI colour.** On a security dashboard, lime green reads as "pass" and would collide with the `--ds-ok` status green (`#3fa876`). Keep product UI on its own semantic palette. The logo's accent and the product's status colours are separate systems.
- On photography or busy backgrounds, put the logo on a solid Ink or Paper panel.
- Single-colour reproduction (embossing, etching, one-colour print, a single-filament 3D print): use `quirk-mark-mono.svg`. The error point keeps its offset, so the meaning survives without colour.
- For a two-material 3D print, print the error point in Signal and everything else in Ink.

## 6. Typography

| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | **Custom-drawn**, not a font | Always use the SVG. Never retype "QU.I.R.K." in a font as a logo |
| Descriptor | JetBrains Mono 600, uppercase | Stretched to the exact width of U.I.R.K. It is the face the dashboard already bundles (`quirk/dashboard/static/fonts/`) |
| Data, code, CLI | JetBrains Mono | Unchanged |

The descriptor in the SVGs is live text. Where JetBrains Mono may not be installed (print vendors, PowerPoint), convert it to outlines before sending the file out.

### Writing the name

- In running text: **QU.I.R.K.** (with all four periods).
- For the package, CLI and code: `quirk`, `quirk-scanner`.
- Avoid "Quirk", "QUIRK" and "Q.U.I.R.K." in published material.
- Spell out **Quantum Infrastructure Readiness Kit** on first use in a document.

## 7. Co-branding in client deliverables

QU.I.R.K. is the instrument, and the client report is the consultancy's deliverable.

- **Report covers belong to the consultancy or client.** `report.branding.logo_path` stays the cover logo. Do not replace it with the QU.I.R.K. logo.
- QU.I.R.K. appears as a small **"Generated by" primary logo** in the report footer or colophon, at least 24 px tall, in Ink or Paper only.
- On a co-branded slide, QU.I.R.K. never sits larger than the partner's logo.

---

## 8. Don'ts

- Don't place the mark next to a wordmark that has its own Q (no "Q QU.I.R.K.").
- Don't recentre the error point onto its node. A centred point means "no error" and breaks the concept.
- Don't replace the 256-gon with a true circle, and don't remove the open seam.
- Don't use Signal anywhere except the error point.
- Don't add gradients, glows, drop shadows, bevels or outline strokes.
- Don't stretch, skew, rotate or re-proportion the lens and the letters independently.
- Don't use the lattice-bearing mark below 48 px. Switch to the favicon.
- Don't use the hero's dial and lattice field at small sizes. At small sizes they turn into noise.
- Don't set the descriptor in a different face or tracking.
